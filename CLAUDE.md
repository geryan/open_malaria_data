## Project

"How open is malaria data? A review of data sharing in *Malaria Journal*". Dr Gerard Ryan (PI) and Dr Katharine Senior, University of Melbourne.

- **Phase 1** (this repo): classify the data availability statement (DAS) of published articles. Current scope is a pilot on *Malaria Journal* volume 24 (published 2025, 457 items). The plan is 10 years of the journal plus smaller samples from comparator journals (not yet chosen).
- **Phase 2**: email corresponding authors whose statement says data are "available on request", then survey non-sharers. This needs University of Melbourne human ethics approval. Draft application documents are in `../ethics/`. **Nothing in this repo may contact authors.**

## Data sources and definitions

- **Volume, not dates.** BMC publishes continuously and the volume equals the year. Crossref has no volume filter, so `01` pulls a wide date window and keeps `target_volume`. Volume 24 runs from 2025-01-05 to 2025-12-29, articles 1–457. 68 items published online in Nov–Dec 2025 carry volume 25 and are excluded.
- `malariajournal.biomedcentral.com/articles/{doi}` redirects to `link.springer.com/article/{doi}`. Publisher pages are the version of record and the primary source for article type (`citation_article_type`), authors (`citation_author` meta, "Surname, Given"), affiliations, emails, and the statement.
- Europe PMC JATS is the second source. It marks corresponding authors but mostly has **no email addresses**. The statements from the two sources agree for all 442 items that have both, after removing case, punctuation and `doi.org` prefixes.
- **Statement headings vary and are sometimes misspelt** ("Availiability of data materials", "Available of data and materials", "Data Availibility"). `das_heading_re` in `00_config.R` is deliberately loose (`avail\w*`). Check unmatched items whenever a new volume is added.
- 12 items in volume 24 have no statement: 11 corrections, notices, an editorial and an abstract, plus 1 systematic review.
- The corresponding-author flag in `authors_long` matches names first (reordering "Surname, Given"). It falls back to email only when no name matches, because one page gives two authors the same email.

## Coding rules (read before changing anything in 05)

- **One source of truth:** `rules/das_coding_rules.tsv` (columns: code, kind, scope, pattern, unless, require, description). It was generated by `python/make_rules.py`. If you edit the TSV directly, either update `make_rules.py` to match or delete it; do not let the two diverge silently.
- **Engine** (`05_code_das.R`; `python/build_pilot.py` uses identical semantics). For each rule:
  - `scope = statement`: the whole statement must match `pattern`, must not match `unless`, and must match `require`.
  - `scope = sentence`: any single sentence must satisfy those three conditions.
  - A code is TRUE if any of its rules fire.
  - Matching is case-insensitive PCRE (`grepl(perl = TRUE, ignore.case = TRUE)`).
  - The sentence splitter is a regex with lookbehinds for "Suppl.", "et al.", "Fig.", "e.g.", "i.e.", "Dr.", "No.".
- Read the TSV with `read.delim(quote = "", comment.char = "", colClasses = "character", na.strings = character(0))`. Patterns contain backslashes. **Never open and save the TSV in Excel.**
- **Categories** (multi-label): `repository`, `in_article`, `on_request`, `restricted_third_party`, `not_available`, `not_applicable`.
- **Derived fields:** `no_statement`, `unclassified`, `n_categories`, `categories`, `f_contradiction`, `f_na_data_article`, and `most_open_route`. `most_open_route` is the first category present in the order repository > in_article > restricted_third_party > on_request > not_available > not_applicable.
- **Flags** (`f_*`) are described in the TSV.
- **Open decisions:**
  - `repository` currently includes public third-party sources (e.g. DHS) when the statement calls them public, although DHS requires registration.
  - 4 statements are `unclassified`: they cite prior publications or DHIS.
  - The coding is the *initial* coder; a human checks every row.
- **After changing rules:** re-run 05–08. `07` will then report differences against the frozen Python pilot (`output/articles.csv`); that is expected. Consider freezing a copy of the previous R output and comparing against that instead.

## Gotchas

- `data/`, `output/` and `browser/` each contain a `.gitignore` of `*`, so nothing in them is tracked. That includes `browser/extract_vol24.js`, which is provenance for the pilot. **Un-ignore that file** (add `!extract_vol24.js` to `browser/.gitignore`). `R/`, `python/` and `rules/` are tracked.
- `squish()` in `00_config.R` holds **literal invisible Unicode spaces** (U+00A0, U+2009, U+202F) inside `"[...]"`. It works in a UTF-8 session but is fragile; replace them with `"[   ]"`.
- The Python pilot CSV writes logicals as `True`/`False`; the R CSVs write `TRUE`/`FALSE`.
- The outputs contain author names and emails. These are public data, but do not push `output/` or `data/` to a public remote.
- Be polite to the publisher: keep `cfg$delay_s` ≥ 1 and the `mailto` for Crossref.

## Likely next steps

1. Human check of the coding in the pilot workbook. Start with the 114 `f_na_data_article` rows: research, methodology and case items that claim no data were generated.
2. Settle the DHS / public-third-party rule.
3. Extend to 10 years (volumes 15–24 = 2016–2025). Parameterise `target_volume` and the date window, loop over volumes, and keep a per-volume cache. Policy changed during this period (see the workbook `policy` sheet):
   - Jan 2016: the journal required deposition in a public repository.
   - Jun 2016: BMC made the "Availability of data and materials" section mandatory.
   - From Jan 2017: "on reasonable request" and "not applicable" templates were offered.
   - Older articles may use the heading "Availability of supporting data", which the heading regex already matches.
4. Comparator journals (not yet chosen).
5. Investigate the fall in "no datasets were generated or analysed" among research articles: about 21–45% each month Jan–Aug 2025, versus 11–20% Sep–Dec 2025 (fig5). Monthly n are 18–55; the cause has not been established.
6. Phase 2 sampling from `on_request` items, only after ethics approval.
